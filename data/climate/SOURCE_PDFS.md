# Original World Climate Simulation PDFs

These are unmodified copies of the original World Climate Simulation documents
downloaded from the [Climate Interactive resources page](https://www.climateinteractive.org/world-climate-simulation-resources/).
The materials are credited to Climate Interactive, MIT Sloan, and the UMass
Lowell Climate Change Initiative; the original documents retain their credits
and notices.

- `materials/`: seven briefing PDFs for the six negotiating regions and the
  separate US Climate Alliance role.
- `supporting/`: the facilitator guide and three supplementary role briefings
  for journalists, climate activists, and the fossil fuel lobby.

The JSON files in `briefs/` contain extracted source text alongside curated
summaries and experimental simulation parameters. The PDFs are the original
source documents; experimental parameters in the JSON are adaptations.
