Simpact · a climate summit, simulated

Watch a summit negotiate itself.

Simpact casts AI agents as real politicians: eight delegations of three. They argue in private, vote on a mandate, then bargain face to face, and every deal moves the thermometer.

Round 1 · Delegations

Eight constellations, talking in private.

Each delegation is a triad: head negotiator, economic minister, climate adviser. They debate, propose a mandate and vote. The round ends with a national position: an annual reduction rate, a peak year, and a pledge to (or request from) the climate fund.

Round 2 · Bilaterals

The triads dissolve. The network begins.

Agents rank who they want to meet across delegations. There are no slots: the moment an agent is free it is paired again, up to five meetings each. Every thread ends one of three ways.

How a run unfolds

Two rounds, one shared sky.

Every turn runs through a game master. Agents only learn what it chooses to show them, so each position and each deal comes from the conversation itself.
