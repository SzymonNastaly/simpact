Team talks

Three players, one dressing room. The head negotiator, economic minister and climate adviser argue it out, put a mandate to a vote, and walk out with a national position: an annual cut, a peak year and a climate-fund number.

 ROUND 2

Fixtures

No fixed slots. Every player ranks who they want to meet across delegations, and the moment one is free they're paired again, up to five meetings each. Every match ends in an agreement, an offer on the table, or a stall.
