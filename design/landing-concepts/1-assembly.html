A climate summit, simulated

The world is negotiating its climate future.

Eight delegations. Twenty-four AI negotiators, each cast as a politician who holds the pen. They deliberate in chambers, bargain in the corridors, and every concession moves the thermometer.

Order of proceedings
  1. I

    In chambers

    Each delegation meets behind closed doors: head negotiator, economic minister, climate adviser. They argue, propose a mandate, vote, and leave with a national position.

  2. II

    The corridors

    Negotiators rank whom they want to meet across the hall. One-to-one talks with no fixed slots: a freed agent is paired again at once, up to five meetings each.

  3. III

    The readout

    Every mandate and every handshake feeds the global model: projected warming, and finance pledged against finance requested.

Round I · Delegation discussion

In chambers

Three voices per flag. Select a delegation in the hall to listen in: who said what, the mandate they voted for, and how far they moved.

Round II · Bilaterals

The corridors

The seats stay put; the conversations cross the floor. Each thread is one meeting between two negotiators, drawn as it starts, coloured by how it ended. No rounds, no slots: whoever is free gets paired again.
